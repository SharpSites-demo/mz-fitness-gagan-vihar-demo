# SharpSites hero improvement SOP

Applies to Nina’s 60 demos and future SharpSites demos. Updated 21 September 2026.

## Non-negotiable visual direction

Use a clear, relevant service scene. Calm, warm, premium, human and trustworthy. Keep the headline short, supporting copy minimal and every button rounded. No arbitrary abstract artwork. The hero image establishes the color palette for the **entire website**: backgrounds, headings, buttons, forms, borders, navigation, footer and decorations. Sample colors from the selected image; do not retain unrelated blue, purple or other template accents. Preserve natural skin tones and authentic business photography.

## Audit and image decision

For every lead record the business, demo URL, category, existing visual, selected source, source attribution, scene, palette and verification result.

1. Keep a suitable existing authentic business photo when the source and business identity are verified and it looks good at desktop and phone sizes.
2. If no suitable verified photo is available in the current source, use a contextual concept illustration. This is not a claim that no business photos exist online.
3. Label imagined scenes as illustrations; never present generated people or premises as actual staff or facilities.
4. Check relevance, image quality, mobile crop, visual palette, headline, controls, contact links and motion.

## Category guidance

| Category | Hero scene |
|---|---|
| Dental | Dentist explains a teeth model to a patient |
| Physiotherapy | Therapist guides a simple movement |
| Doctor / clinic | Clinician listens or explains during a consultation |
| Counselling | Calm seated conversation in a reassuring room |
| ENT | Specialist explains an ear model |
| Women’s health / wellness | Private, supportive consultation |
| Skin clinic | Consultation with a mirror and clear human interaction |
| Wedding / banquet / farmhouse | Decor planning or a tasteful celebration scene |
| Gym | Guided strength training with credible equipment |
| Car dealer | Relevant vehicle in an appropriate showroom |
| Luxury watches | Product-first watch scene with realistic proportions |
| Clothing showroom | Relevant garments and a refined retail setting |

## Shared hero system

Short headline, business name and locality, existing attributed review proof, one primary enquiry action, one supporting direction action, and a large contextual image. Stack cleanly on phones. Use generous spacing, soft corners and sufficient text contrast. Preserve verified business facts and contact destinations.

## Motion

Current release: gentle nine-second image drift or floating photo frame, a visible pause control, off-screen pausing and reduced-motion CSS. Keep text and buttons stable. This is **not** character animation.

For actual guided arm/exercise motion, create a short restrained video or properly rigged illustration, inspect anatomy and timing, add a still poster and pause control, and test mobile loading and reduced-motion behavior. That feature remains pending. Google Vids access was blocked at the account/sign-in approval step. Do not retry that boundary without explicit approval. No Gumloop AI credits or external paid video generation are authorized.

## Execution and verification

Keep original source backups and a per-lead image/palette manifest. Build locally; preserve phone and WhatsApp targets. Stage representative categories, check mobile/tablet/desktop, then publish each demo and retain its commit result. Verify the public HTML matches the intended revision. Check image loading, overflow, rounded controls, anchor links, one floating contact button and pause behavior. Record the actual scope and failures; never label an inherited 116-check audit or Lighthouse score as passed without running it.

The 21 September release uses 58 contextual scene illustrations and preserves 2 existing business photos. The websites remain independent design previews. Existing owner-content placeholders were retained.
